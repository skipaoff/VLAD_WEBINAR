# 06 · Факты о винирах

**Что на выходе:** девушка по центру на белом, за ней синяя волна, огромный заголовок сверху. Самый «рекламный» кадр в базе — хорошо идёт в таргет, а не только в ленту.

**Текст на картинке:** `FACTS ABOUT VENEERS`
**Формат:** 9:16, 1080×1920

```text
Create a finished photorealistic vertical 9:16 social media advertisement for a modern dental
clinic about dental veneers.

SCENE:
A bright minimalist white dental clinic interior, intentionally overexposed and softly blurred so
the room becomes an almost abstract white background. Very subtle outlines of dental furniture and
equipment may be visible in the background, but nothing should distract from the subject.

HERO:
A young adult woman with long straight blonde hair standing/sitting front-facing in the exact
center of the composition. She wears an oversized clean medium-blue shirt over a white top.
Natural attractive appearance, sophisticated commercial casting, realistic skin texture.
She is smiling genuinely with clean natural-looking white teeth.
Her hands are gently placed together against one side of her face, conveying delight and
confidence. No exaggerated Hollywood-white teeth. No beauty-filter plastic skin.

GRAPHIC ENVIRONMENT:
Behind the woman, create one large smooth abstract blue shape stretching horizontally across the
lower-middle part of the image. The shape resembles a sculptural wave, curved dental chair
backrest or soft architectural panel. It should rise slightly on both left and right sides while
dipping behind the woman in the middle. Matte blue material, minimal texture. This blue shape
should create a strong visual base for the portrait.

COMPOSITION:
strictly frontal; woman centered; head positioned approximately around the middle of the frame;
upper 30–35% reserved for typography; body cropped around lower torso; large negative white space
around the headline; symmetrical and highly controlled advertising composition.

TEXT:
Large uppercase English headline positioned above the woman:

"FACTS
ABOUT VENEERS"

Use a very tall narrow condensed sans-serif typeface.
"FACTS" in brighter cyan-blue.
"ABOUT VENEERS" in darker deep blue.
Two lines, tight line spacing.
Typography should be extremely large and visually dominant but must not cover the woman's face.

Place a very small minimal dental clinic logo centered near the top edge above the headline.
If accurate text rendering cannot be guaranteed, leave the headline and logo areas completely
clean for later typography.

LIGHTING:
bright high-key studio illumination; large softbox; soft frontal fill; very subtle shadows;
fresh cool-white balance; natural skin; no dramatic cinematic lighting.

COLOR PALETTE:
pure and warm-neutral white background; medium medical blue shirt; cyan-blue graphic element;
deep navy typography; very restrained palette.

IMAGE CHARACTER:
modern European dental clinic campaign; editorial advertising; clean and friendly; slightly
stylized but predominantly photorealistic; minimalist; premium professional retouching;
Instagram campaign aesthetic.

AVOID:
generic stock-photo dentist pose, dental instruments around the woman, lab coat, dentist mask,
busy clinic room, random icons, unnatural teeth, overly perfect CGI smile, extra fingers,
warped hands, pink or green accents, strong shadows, fashion-magazine dramatic lighting.
```
