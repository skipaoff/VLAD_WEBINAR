# 09 · Наши врачи — женский портрет

**Что на выходе:** карточка знакомства с врачом: портрет слева, имя и специальность справа, крупная подпись серии внизу. Собирает доверие лучше любого текста про опыт.

**Текст на картинке:** имя врача + `ORTHODONTIST` + `OUR DOCTORS`
**Формат:** 4:5, 1080×1350
**Нужно от бизнеса:** имя врача и специальность.

```text
Create a finished photorealistic vertical 4:5 social media advertising post introducing a female
orthodontist for a modern dental clinic.

REFERENCE ART DIRECTION:
clean professional dental clinic branding; bright high-key medical portrait photography; minimal
editorial graphic design; white and pale cyan-blue palette; approachable, trustworthy, premium but
not luxury; modern European private dental clinic; strong mobile-first hierarchy; very clean
Instagram advertising aesthetic.

SCENE:
A bright contemporary dental clinic interior rendered extremely softly in the background.
The background should be predominantly white with a subtle pale cyan-blue gradient, especially
toward the upper-left area. Dental equipment may be barely visible as soft blurred silhouettes,
but the environment must remain almost abstract and uncluttered. No obvious hospital atmosphere.
No dark interiors. No busy medical equipment.

MAIN SUBJECT:
A young adult female dentist / orthodontist positioned on the LEFT HALF of the composition.
Portrait from approximately mid-thigh or waist upward. She is turned very slightly toward camera
but essentially front-facing.
She has: neat professional appearance; light brown or strawberry-blonde hair pulled back into a
clean low bun or ponytail; thin elegant transparent or dark-rimmed optical glasses; natural
professional makeup; realistic healthy skin texture; warm confident smile; natural-looking white
teeth.
She wears a crisp modern white dental uniform with a high Mandarin-style medical collar.
No visible branding on the uniform.

POSE:
Relaxed upright posture. One arm naturally relaxed. In the other hand she holds one slim metallic
dental instrument, such as a dental mirror or examination probe, near chest level. The instrument
should be clearly identifiable but visually secondary.
Her expression should communicate: professional, friendly, calm, competent, trustworthy.
Avoid exaggerated posing.

PORTRAIT LIGHTING:
very bright diffused studio lighting; large frontal softbox effect; gentle fill from both sides;
almost no harsh shadows; slightly cool white balance; natural skin tone; soft realistic highlights
on glasses and metal instrument; premium commercial portrait retouching.
Do not make skin plastic or over-smoothed.

COMPOSITION:
vertical 4:5; female doctor occupies approximately 48–52% of total frame; subject placed
left-of-center; her head around the upper-middle portion of frame; large clean negative space on
the RIGHT SIDE for information; bottom 20–25% reserved for large campaign title.
Keep generous breathing room around all graphic elements.

TYPOGRAPHY LAYOUT:
TOP RIGHT: reserve a small clean area for a minimal dental clinic logo. Very small scale.
Dark gray and pale blue. Lots of negative space around it.

RIGHT CENTER: place doctor information in English transliteration, exactly:
"{{DOCTOR NAME}}"
Up to three lines. Dark charcoal-gray. Clean geometric sans-serif. Medium weight. Left aligned.
Moderate line spacing.
Directly below the name create a compact rectangular BLUE LABEL containing:
"ORTHODONTIST"
White condensed sans-serif lettering. The blue label should be approximately the same width as the
name block or slightly wider. Flat medium medical blue. Very subtle or no shadow.

BOTTOM AREA: large campaign title:
"OUR
DOCTORS"
designed as two distinct treatments:
"OUR" — large outlined uppercase letters, white or transparent fill, medium-blue stroke, tall
condensed sans-serif.
"DOCTORS" — directly underneath, large solid medium-blue uppercase letters, same condensed
typeface, heavy weight.
The bottom typography should span roughly 50–55% of the image width and be positioned mostly
toward the RIGHT HALF, balancing the doctor on the left. Use tight vertical spacing.

If accurate text generation is unreliable: preserve the exact empty typography zones, create the
blue specialty rectangle without lettering if necessary, and leave all final typography to be
added later.

GRAPHIC STYLE:
minimalist; clean; medical; editorial; structured grid; no unnecessary decoration; no icons except
the clinic logo; no floating medical symbols; no circles; no excessive gradients.

COLOR PALETTE:
#FFFFFF dominant white; very pale cyan-blue background; medium medical blue graphic accents;
charcoal gray body text; natural skin tones; silver dental instrument.

CAMERA:
commercial portrait photography; 70–85 mm portrait lens feel; eye-level camera; very slight
shallow depth of field; doctor perfectly sharp; background softly blurred; minimal perspective
distortion.

IMAGE QUALITY:
photorealistic; high-end clinic advertising; correct human anatomy; realistic hands; realistic
glasses; correct dental instrument; clean fabric texture; natural teeth; sharp facial details;
subtle commercial retouching.

AVOID:
generic stock-photo dentist, arms crossed pose, clipboard, stethoscope, surgical mask, blue
disposable cap, exaggerated Hollywood smile, unnaturally white teeth, plastic skin, dark shadows,
hospital corridor, busy treatment room, random tooth icons, floating dental instruments, neon
blue, luxury gold, 3D typography, overly glossy CGI, extra fingers, warped hands, incorrect
glasses, malformed dental tools, text covering the face, too much information.

FINAL FEEL:
A clean, reassuring and highly polished introduction of a real doctor from an established
contemporary dental clinic. The design should feel calm, competent and human, with the doctor's
portrait as the emotional focal point and the typography acting as part of a carefully structured
brand system.
```
