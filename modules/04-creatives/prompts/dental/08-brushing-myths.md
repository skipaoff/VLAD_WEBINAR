# 08 · Мифы о чистке зубов

**Что на выходе:** четыре зубные щётки стоят как колонны, за ними гигантское слово. Самый необычный кадр в базе — им хорошо разбавлять ленту из портретов.

**Текст на картинке:** `MYTHS` + плашка `ABOUT BRUSHING TEETH`
**Формат:** 9:16, 1080×1920

```text
Create a finished vertical 9:16 commercial advertising visual for a contemporary dental clinic
about myths surrounding tooth brushing.

CORE CONCEPT:
Transform four different toothbrushes into monumental vertical architectural columns.
The toothbrushes should stand upright at different depths like a minimalist installation in an
art gallery.

BACKGROUND:
almost pure off-white studio background with a very subtle warm-to-cool neutral gradient;
clean floor and wall blend almost seamlessly together; no visible clinic clutter.

OBJECTS:
four upright toothbrushes placed across the scene from left to right.
1. Far-left foreground: a very dark charcoal or black electric toothbrush handle, extremely close
   to camera, partially cropped by the left edge, strongly out of focus due to shallow depth of
   field.
2. Left-center: a slim blue manual toothbrush standing vertically, medium distance, visible
   bristle head, mostly sharp.
3. Right-center: a slim white electric or sonic toothbrush with a compact oval brush head,
   standing farther back, sharp.
4. Far-right: a taller white manual or electric toothbrush, visible dark bristle head, slightly
   behind the main composition.
The toothbrushes should appear dramatically oversized, like architectural pillars, but maintain
completely believable proportions and materials.

TYPOGRAPHY:
Place an enormous English headline behind and partially between the toothbrushes:
"MYTHS"
very large uppercase condensed sans-serif letters, bright medical blue, occupying almost the full
width of the image. Several toothbrush handles and brush heads should physically overlap the
typography so the image has genuine depth.
Below the large word, place a horizontal saturated-blue rounded rectangular label containing:
"ABOUT BRUSHING TEETH"
uppercase narrow condensed white lettering.
The blue label should sit approximately across the middle-lower part of the word "MYTHS" and
slightly overlap the toothbrushes.
If perfect text is not possible, leave the exact typography area clear and create only the visual
hierarchy and blue label shape.

CAMERA:
low-to-neutral frontal camera angle, similar to architectural photography, 50–70 mm lens feel,
shallow depth of field; left foreground toothbrush strongly blurred; middle objects tack-sharp;
far background slightly softened.

LIGHT:
clean soft studio lighting from upper-left/front; subtle elongated contact shadows at the bases of
the toothbrushes; soft reflections on plastic handles; very bright but not blown out.

DESIGN:
minimal Swiss-inspired advertising layout; high contrast between huge typography and simple
objects; smart use of occlusion; clean modern medical branding; one strong concept; almost no
decoration.

COLOR PALETTE:
white, off-white, cyan blue, medium medical blue, one charcoal-black foreground accent.

AVOID:
toothpaste, foam, water splashes, bathroom environment, people, floating toothbrushes, random
dental icons, cartoon aesthetic, neon, complex backgrounds, excessive shadows, warped bristles,
duplicated brush heads, random text, watermarks.
```
