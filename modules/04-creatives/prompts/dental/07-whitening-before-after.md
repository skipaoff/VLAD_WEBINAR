# 07 · Отбеливание: до и после

**Что на выходе:** улыбка крупным планом, разделённая пополам — слева свой оттенок, справа после отбеливания. Разница видна без единого слова, поэтому пост работает даже в ленте на пролистывании.

**Текст на картинке:** `TEETH WHITENING`
**Формат:** 4:5, 1080×1350

```text
Create a finished photorealistic vertical 4:5 commercial dental advertisement illustrating
professional teeth whitening through one bold surreal visual metaphor.

BACKGROUND:
A minimalist pale powder-blue dental clinic environment. The upper half is a flat clean blue wall
with only extremely subtle blurred dental details. The bottom portion transitions into a clean
white dental surface or chair. Clinical but elegant. Very few objects.

MAIN HERO OBJECT:
In the exact center of the frame place a large rectangular rounded-corner photographic panel
containing only the lower central part of a smiling human face: nose mostly outside the crop,
pink lips, mouth, upper and lower teeth, slight surrounding skin. The smile panel should feel like
a physical glossy photographic object standing in space rather than simply an image pasted on top.

BEFORE / AFTER EFFECT:
Divide the smile exactly vertically through its center with an extremely thin subtle line.
LEFT HALF: slightly warmer natural ivory teeth; realistic, healthy, but visibly less white.
RIGHT HALF: professionally whitened bright natural teeth; clean neutral white enamel; do not make
the teeth unnaturally pure white or fluorescent.
Keep the exact same lips, face and geometry on both halves so it clearly reads as a
before-and-after comparison. The difference should be immediately visible without labels.

HEADLINE:
Above the smile panel, place a large floating white rounded rectangle with soft corners and a very
subtle shadow. Inside it place the English text:
"TEETH
WHITENING"
uppercase tall condensed sans-serif. First line cyan/medium blue. Second line darker blue.
Large but clean. Centered.
If text accuracy cannot be guaranteed, create the white headline plaque blank and preserve enough
space to add text later.

COMPOSITION:
vertical 4:5; central symmetry; headline approximately around upper-middle; smile hero around
center; large amount of clean white negative space in lower third.

DEPTH DETAILS:
Add one heavily out-of-focus tooth-shaped dental object entering the upper-left foreground.
Add another very subtle blurred white dental object entering the lower-right corner.
They should act purely as depth framing devices. Do not make them visually important.

LIGHT:
soft high-key commercial studio light; very gentle shadows; cool daylight white balance; smooth
reflections on the headline plaque; realistic lips and teeth; premium cosmetic advertising
retouching.

PALETTE:
powder blue, white, natural skin, soft pink lips, cyan-blue typography.

STYLE:
modern dental campaign, clean art direction, subtle surrealism, photorealistic product
photography, 3D spatial composition, minimal graphic design, very polished but not overly CGI.

AVOID:
arrows, "before" and "after" text, yellow exaggerated teeth, fake porcelain-looking teeth, open
mouth dentistry procedure, instruments touching teeth, blood, busy props, cartoon teeth,
oversaturated blue, harsh shadows, random lettering, distorted smile.
```
