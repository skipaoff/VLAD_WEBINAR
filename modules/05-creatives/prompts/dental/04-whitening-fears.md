# 04 · Страхи при отбеливании

**Что на выходе:** кадр самой процедуры с синей лампой, снизу синяя плашка с заголовком. Пост-возражение: снимает страх до того, как человек спросит.

**Текст на картинке:** `FEARS ABOUT TEETH WHITENING`
**Формат:** 4:5, 1080×1350

```text
Create a vertical Instagram post, 4:5, 1080x1350, in a clean dental educational style with a
stronger photo-led composition.

Composition:
* full-frame realistic close-up dental procedure photo
* show a patient receiving a teeth-whitening procedure
* include visible whitening lamp or curing device emitting cool blue/violet light
* patient may wear protective orange glasses
* keep the image clinical but visually appealing, non-graphic
* overlay the lower 25–35% of the image with a blue semi-transparent or solid gradient block

Typography:
* in the lower-left area, over the blue block, place the English text exactly:
"FEARS
ABOUT TEETH WHITENING"
* use condensed sans-serif uppercase
* white text
* the word "FEARS" should be larger and more dominant
* the second line smaller underneath

Style:
* informative, reassuring
* high-key medical photography
* cool white + blue color palette
* no blood, pain, or disturbing visuals
* no cluttered medical scene
* no extra icons unless very subtle

The final result should resemble a modern educational Instagram post about whitening concerns.
```
