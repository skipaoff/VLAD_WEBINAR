# 01 · Улыбка заражает

**Что на выходе:** портрет улыбающейся девушки на светло-голубом фоне, крупный заголовок сверху, много воздуха. Пост-настроение, не про услугу — чтобы лента не выглядела как прайс.

**Текст на картинке:** `A SMILE IS «CONTAGIOUS» :)`
**Формат:** 4:5, 1080×1350

```text
Create a vertical Instagram post, 4:5, 1080x1350, in a clean modern dental social media style.
Design a promotional post for a dental clinic with a soft pale blue and white color palette,
minimal medical aesthetic, flat graphic elements, and realistic photography.

Composition:
* light icy-blue background
* in the lower-left to center area place a realistic portrait of a young smiling woman, friendly
  and approachable, in a white t-shirt, medium shot, slightly turned toward camera, hands softly
  folded near face
* behind her place a large blue circular graphic shape
* add very subtle oversized translucent background lettering or abstract shapes for depth
* keep the layout airy and clean

Typography:
* headline at the top-left / upper area
* use condensed sans-serif uppercase typography
* text in English, exactly:
"A SMILE IS
«CONTAGIOUS» :)"
* emphasize the word «CONTAGIOUS» in a darker, bolder blue
* the rest of the headline in lighter blue tones
* typography should feel playful but professional

Style:
* modern dental clinic branding
* minimal, fresh, approachable
* realistic photo, flat vector shapes
* no clutter
* no extra icons except subtle design accents
* no stocky corporate look
* no 3D cartoon tooth

The final result should look like a professionally designed Instagram post for a dental clinic,
with balanced white space and clear mobile readability.
```
