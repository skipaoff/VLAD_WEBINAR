# 10 · Наши врачи — мужской портрет

**Что на выходе:** врач по центру, синяя полоса со специальностью проходит за ним, подпись серии внизу. Парная карточка к девятой — из них собирается раздел «команда».

**Текст на картинке:** `DENTAL SURGEON` + имя врача + `OUR DOCTORS`
**Формат:** 4:5, 1080×1350
**Нужно от бизнеса:** имя врача и специальность.

```text
Create a finished photorealistic vertical 4:5 social media advertising post introducing a male
dental surgeon for a contemporary dental clinic.

ART DIRECTION:
bright minimalist dental branding; premium commercial medical portrait photography; white high-key
environment; clean cyan-blue graphic accents; modern editorial grid; friendly and trustworthy
rather than cold; minimal social media campaign aesthetic; professional European private clinic.

BACKGROUND:
Create an extremely bright white modern dental clinic interior. A dental chair and treatment
equipment may be faintly visible in the background, but they should be heavily desaturated, softly
blurred and almost disappear into the white environment. Use a subtle white-to-very-light-cool-gray
gradient. The upper background should remain especially clean to provide breathing space around
the logo and doctor.

MAIN SUBJECT:
A male dentist / dental surgeon in his early-to-mid 30s positioned slightly RIGHT OF CENTER.
Portrait approximately waist-up. He is facing directly toward the camera.
Appearance: short neat dark brown hair; clean-shaven or extremely subtle stubble; warm natural
smile; confident relaxed expression; realistic healthy skin; natural white teeth; professional but
approachable appearance.

CLOTHING:
clean white medical coat or dental uniform; light-colored shirt underneath; small dark accent from
undershirt or collar may be visible; white disposable medical gloves.
No surgical mask. No cap. No goggles.

POSE:
Both hands raised naturally around chest level. He may hold a small dental model, dental mirror,
surgical dental instrument or demonstrate a small object with his gloved hands. Hands should feel
natural and relaxed, not theatrical. The gesture should communicate professional expertise and
dexterity.

MAIN GRAPHIC DEVICE:
Create one wide horizontal MEDIUM-BLUE RECTANGULAR BAND entering from the LEFT EDGE of the image
and extending approximately 65–70% across the frame. Position it slightly above the vertical
center of the composition.
The blue band should pass BEHIND THE DOCTOR'S BODY. This overlap is essential: the graphic band
exists in the background layer; the doctor's shoulders, head and arms remain in front of it; this
creates genuine editorial depth.
The band should have clean straight edges. No gradient. No glow. No heavy shadow.
Inside the left part of the blue band place the English text:
"DENTAL
SURGEON"
Two lines. Uppercase. White bold geometric or condensed sans-serif. Left aligned. Generous inner
padding. Strong readability. The text should occupy approximately the left half of the band and
stop before reaching the doctor's face.

DOCTOR NAME:
Directly UNDER THE BLUE BAND, on the left side, place:
"{{DOCTOR NAME}}"
Up to three compact lines. Small dark charcoal-gray sans-serif. Left aligned. Much smaller than
the specialty title.
Visual hierarchy: 1. doctor portrait, 2. specialty, 3. campaign title, 4. name, 5. logo.

LOGO:
Small minimalist dental clinic logo in the upper-left portion of the frame. Place it well above
the blue band. Give it generous white negative space. Dark gray / light blue only.

BOTTOM TITLE:
Near the bottom center of the image place:
"OUR DOCTORS"
One line. Large uppercase medium-blue letters. Tall clean sans-serif or condensed grotesk. Bold
but not extremely heavy. Centered horizontally. Leave a generous white margin below it.
This title should feel like the recurring name of a branded content series.

TYPOGRAPHIC STYLE:
clean modern sans-serif; slightly condensed; medical editorial design; uppercase for all primary
information; no serif; no italics; no decorative effects; no heavy drop shadows.
If precise text cannot be rendered reliably: generate the composition with blank text zones,
preserve the blue horizontal band, leave the bottom headline zone clean, and add all typography in
post-production.

COMPOSITION:
vertical 4:5; doctor slightly right of center; doctor occupies approximately 45–50% of image
width; head approximately in upper-middle region; blue band cuts horizontally across the middle
third; left-side text block balances the portrait; large clean white area at top; campaign
headline centered at bottom.
Use intentional asymmetry: visual weight of the doctor on right balanced by the blue information
band and name on left.

LIGHTING:
soft high-key clinical photography; large diffused frontal light; soft lateral fill; minimal
shadow density; neutral-to-cool white balance; realistic specular highlights on gloves and
instruments; bright whites without clipping all fabric texture.

CAMERA:
professional commercial portrait; 70–85mm lens look; camera at eye level; slightly shallow depth
of field; doctor and hands sharp; clinic environment subtly blurred; no wide-angle distortion.

PALETTE:
white 75–80%; pale neutral gray 5–10%; medical cyan-blue 10–15%; charcoal typography; natural skin
tones.

TEXTURE:
clean cotton medical fabric; slightly matte nitrile gloves; realistic metallic dental tools; soft
skin texture; subtle photographic grain; natural commercial retouching.

AVOID:
hospital stock-photo aesthetic, medical emergency atmosphere, operating room, blood, visible
procedures, patients, surgical mask, crossed arms, clipboard, stethoscope, bright colorful
equipment, random dental icons, tooth illustrations, floating objects, overly futuristic clinic,
blue neon lighting, dark shadows, dramatic cinema grading, fake porcelain skin, over-white teeth,
warped fingers, extra hands, broken medical instruments, incorrect dentistry equipment, text
overlapping face, excessive graphic elements.

FINAL RESULT:
A polished branded doctor-introduction post for a modern dental clinic. It should look like
professional agency work combining real commercial portrait photography with restrained editorial
graphic design. The viewer should instantly understand: this is one of the clinic's doctors, this
is their specialty, and the clinic presents its medical team as competent, modern and approachable.
```
